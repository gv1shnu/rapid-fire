THE LOST SCHEMA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

The Lost Schema is a non-commercial educational web application used to run rapid-fire SQL practice sessions inside a university Database Management Systems (DBMS) lab. This policy explains what information the application handles when you sign in and play, why, and how it is protected. Using the application means you understand this policy.

Who can use it. Access is limited to members of the college whose Google Workspace accounts belong to the rishihood.edu.in or nst.rishihood.edu.in domains. Accounts from any other domain, and non-Google logins, are rejected at sign-in.

Information we collect

  • Google account identity. When you sign in with Google we receive, through the requested openid email profile scopes, your email address, your basic profile name, and a Google account identifier. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google service.
  • Class roster details. Your instructor provisions a roster of participating email addresses and their assigned lab section. Your email is matched against that roster to place you in the correct section.
  • Gameplay data you create. A display nickname and avatar seed you choose, the answer you select for each question, and server-measured timing, per-question and per-round scores, streaks and accuracy metrics for each round you play.

How the information is used

  • To authenticate you and confirm you belong to an allowed domain.
  • To place you in your instructor-assigned lab section.
  • To run the game fairly — one attempt per student, a shared countdown, and scoring revealed only at the debrief.
  • To give your instructor class-level and per-student performance reports after a round closes, so the lab can be reviewed and graded.

Your answers, scores and metrics are never sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Who can see your data

  • You see your own debrief after each round finishes.
  • The instructor who runs your session can see the scores and metrics for that session's students. The database enforces that only the assigned, allowlisted instructor of a session can read its reports; other students, other instructors, and anonymous callers cannot.
  • During a live round, scores and correct answers stay sealed from everyone until the debrief.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in the application's Supabase (PostgreSQL) project. Row-level security is enabled on every table and the browser has no direct read or write access; all access goes through a small set of audited server functions. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth using Google as the sole identity provider.

Retention and deletion

Session and score records are kept only as long as needed for the course — typically the current term — and may be removed after grades are finalised. To ask what is held about you, or to request correction or deletion, contact the DBMS teaching team that runs your lab (see below). Removing your account from the roster prevents further sign-in.

Children and eligibility

The application is intended for enrolled university students and staff. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the application evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

Contact
